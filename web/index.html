Sources & method

Days ahead is the number of calendar days between making a reservation and arriving. Zero means the same day. The middle half falls between the 25th and 75th percentiles. The bars show the percentage of matching reservations at each park.

These historical reservations describe successful bookings. They do not measure vacancies, turned-away campers or when a campsite sold out. Counts include ordinary campsites with at least one booked night, a recorded tent or RV, and a nonnegative lead time.
